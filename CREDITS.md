
## Generated sounds
All sounds in mod/sound/sigf/ were generated with fal.ai ElevenLabs models, then converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono WAV:
- Sound effects (ollie_pop, land, grind, roll, bail, crowd_ooh, cheer, trick_win): fal-ai/elevenlabs/sound-effects/v2
- Music (music, 30 s instrumental skate-punk loop): elevenlabs/music/v2.5
- Voice lines (v_sick, v_gnarly, v_nailed, v_ouch): fal-ai/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v3
- Total cost: about $0.33 USD (SFX ~$0.024, music $0.30, voices ~$0.005)

## Generated art
All original, generated with fal (no real logos or brands). Converted with kits/portal2/texture.py to mod/materials/sigf/.
- gen/title.png (material sigf/title), gen/tag_sick.png (sigf/tag_sick), gen/tag_skate.png (sigf/tag_skate): generated with fal, model fal-ai/nano-banana-2
- gen/xray.png (sigf/xray), gen/skull.png (sigf/skull), gen/burst.png (sigf/burst), gen/lightning.png (sigf/lightning), gen/star.png (sigf/star): generated with fal, model fal-ai/flux/schnell
- gen/redflash.png (sigf/redflash): procedurally drawn with Python/PIL (no AI)
Total fal cost: about $0.27 (3 x $0.08 + 7 x $0.003).

## Game assets reused
All 3D models (floor plates, rails, Wheatley personality spheres, turret, wheels) and the test chamber map are Portal 2's own files, loaded from the player's own copy of the game. Nothing was ripped from any skateboarding game: the trick names are generic, the code, art and sounds are original.
