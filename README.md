# Skate 3 Takeover

**Portal 2** × Skate 3 · visual · idea by the team

Bring Skate 3 into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned a Portal 2 test chamber into a neon skate park: portal-air tricks, rail grinds, and Wheatley bailing into the Hall of Meat.

## Install

Copy the mod folder's contents into a new folder named sigf inside your Portal 2 folder, then start Portal 2 with the launch option -game sigf +sv_cheats 1 +map sp_a2_catapult_intro. The mod loads on its own: move the mouse to take over the board, W/S for speed, Space to ollie.
